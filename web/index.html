Wing Commander

The 1990 DOS game, statically recompiled to WebAssembly. Bring your own game files (GOG release).

Drop your Wing Commander folder here (the one with WC.EXE and GAMEDAT).

    Game files and save games stay in this browser (IndexedDB); nothing is uploaded anywhere. Save games made here are kept when you replace the game files. In the game: ⌘/Ctrl+F full screen, ⌘/Ctrl+↑/↓ speed (in flight the frame rate), ⌘/Ctrl+←/→ mouse speed in full screen. Logs go to the browser console.
